Questions

Do I need to be good to enter?

No. First-timers have their own leaderboard and their own prize, and everyone gets a scorecard showing where to improve.

Who can enter?

Any student enrolled at a Canadian university or college, including international students. We check your school email and may ask for proof of enrolment.

Can I enter with friends?

You can all enter, but you build alone. Every result belongs to one person, which is what makes it worth something to an employer.

Where does my {{ event.price_label }} go?

30% goes straight into that Open's prize pool and 10% into the season prize. The rest runs judging, the platform and the rewards.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund until {{ event.refund_deadline.strftime("%B %-d") }}. Email {{ settings.contact_email }} from the address you registered with.

What if I miss an Open?

Your best three of four Opens count toward the season standings, so one missed weekend doesn't end your season.
