Official Rules

Deadlock {{ event.full_name }}. Last updated {{ legal_updated }}.

1. Organizer

The {{ event.name }} ("the Open") is run by {{ settings.legal_entity_label }}, {{ settings.mailing_address_label }} ("Deadlock", "we"). Contact: {{ settings.contact_email }}.

2. Who can enter

  • You must be enrolled at a Canadian university or college when you register and during the Open, and be at least 18 years old. We don't accept entries from anyone under 18.
  • Entries are individual. You may not work in a team or get help from another person.
  • We verify enrolment through your school email and may ask for proof of enrolment. We may disqualify an entry we can't verify.
  • Deadlock staff, judges, problem owners and their immediate families are not eligible. [Counsel to confirm the full exclusion list.]

3. How to enter

Registering your interest at {{ settings.site_url }}/register is free and doesn't enter you in anything. We'll email everyone who registered their interest when entries open. To enter, you'll pay the entry fee of {{ event.price_label }} CAD through Stripe, using your school email, before entries close. One entry per person; if you pay twice, we refund the extra entry.

The entry fee is a one-time payment for one Open. It is not a subscription, nothing renews automatically, and we will never charge you again unless you choose to enter another Open.

4. Schedule

  • The brief is released {{ event.starts_at.strftime("%A, %B %-d, %Y at %-I:%M %p") }} ET.
  • Submissions close {{ event.ends_at.strftime("%A, %B %-d, %Y at %-I:%M %p") }} ET. Late submissions are not judged.

5. What you submit

A demo video of three minutes or less, a link to your code repository, a list of the languages, services and AI tools you used, and a one-page decision log. Your repository's first commit must be after the brief is released.

6. Use of AI tools

You may use any AI tool. Your decision log must describe how you used AI. You must be able to explain your code: top-ranked entrants complete a short private walkthrough with a judge before results are final.

7. Judging

This is a contest of skill. Winners are decided by judges using the criteria below. There is no random draw and no element of chance.

  • Solves the brief: 30 points, half from the problem owner's held-out test cases and half from judges
  • Technical craft: 30 points
  • Product judgement: 20 points
  • Demo clarity: 20 points

Ties are broken by the "Solves the brief" score, then by technical craft. Judges' decisions are final.

8. Prizes

  • The cash prize pool is 30% of net entry fees for this Open plus any sponsor top-ups. Because it depends on how many people enter, its size is not fixed in advance; we'll publish the amount before results.
  • The pool is split among the top three entrants: 50%, 30% and 20%. [Confirm split.]
  • Other rewards (merch such as engraved keycaps and custom keyboards, mentorship sessions, Builder Cards) are non-cash and have no cash value. Mentors are volunteers and sessions depend on their availability.
  • Winners must answer a skill-testing question and confirm eligibility before receiving a prize. [Counsel to confirm.]
  • Prizes are paid within 30 days of results day.

9. Ownership

You own the code and work you submit. You give Deadlock permission to show your submission, name, province and results on our website and social channels. You may choose to share your work with the problem owner under an open licence; this is optional.

The Deadlock 10: we publish a profile, video or LinkedIn post about a builder only with that builder's separate written permission. You can say no, or withdraw permission later, without any effect on your rank or prizes.

You must have the right to submit everything in your entry, including any code, data, images or video. You must not submit anything that infringes someone else's rights. Submissions are links to material you host yourself; we don't host them.

10. Disqualification

We may disqualify an entry that breaks these rules, including work by someone other than the entrant, code written before the brief was released, plagiarism, or breaking the Code of Conduct.

11. Refunds

You can get a full refund until {{ event.refund_deadline.strftime("%B %-d, %Y") }} by emailing {{ settings.contact_email }} from the address you registered with. After that date, entry fees are refunded only if we cancel the Open.

12. Copyright and other complaints

If you believe something on this site or in our channels infringes your copyright or other rights, email {{ settings.contact_email }} with the details, including where you saw it and what right you hold. We'll review it promptly and take down or correct anything that is infringing.
